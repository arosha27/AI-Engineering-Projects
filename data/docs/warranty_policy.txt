BRIGHTBYTE ELECTRONICS — WARRANTY POLICY

1. STANDARD WARRANTY COVERAGE
Most products sold by BrightByte carry a manufacturer warranty against defects in materials and workmanship, valid from the date of delivery:
- Smartphones and laptops: 12 months
- Home appliances (refrigerators, washing machines, air conditioners): 24 months
- Small appliances (blenders, irons, kettles): 12 months
- Accessories (cables, chargers, cases): 6 months
- Earphones and headphones: 6 months

2. WHAT IS COVERED
The warranty covers manufacturing defects, hardware malfunctions under normal use, and premature component failure. This includes issues such as screens not turning on, motors failing, or batteries not holding charge significantly below rated capacity.

3. WHAT IS NOT COVERED
Warranty does not cover:
- Physical damage (cracked screens, water damage, drops)
- Damage from unauthorized repairs or modifications
- Normal wear and tear (e.g., battery degradation within expected range)
- Damage from power surges or improper voltage use
- Cosmetic damage that does not affect function

4. HOW TO FILE A WARRANTY CLAIM
Step 1: Contact BrightByte support with your order number and a description of the issue.
Step 2: Support will guide you to either a local authorized service center or arrange a pickup for inspection.
Step 3: If the defect is confirmed as covered, BrightByte will repair, replace, or refund the item at our discretion, typically within 10-15 business days.

5. EXTENDED WARRANTY
Customers may purchase an Extended Warranty Plan at checkout for eligible products, extending coverage by an additional 12 months beyond the standard warranty period, for a fee based on product category.

6. WARRANTY TRANSFERABILITY
Standard warranty is tied to the original proof of purchase and is transferable to a new owner if the original receipt is provided.

7. OUT-OF-WARRANTY REPAIRS
For products outside their warranty period, BrightByte's partner service centers offer paid repair services with quotes provided before any work begins.
