BRIGHTBYTE ELECTRONICS — SHIPPING POLICY

1. SHIPPING METHODS AND TIMELINES
- Standard Shipping: 4-6 business days, free on orders over Rs. 5,000
- Express Shipping: 1-2 business days, flat rate of Rs. 350
- Same-Day Delivery: Available in Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad for orders placed before 12 PM, flat rate Rs. 500

2. ORDER PROCESSING TIME
Orders are processed within 24 hours on business days (Monday-Saturday). Orders placed on Sundays or public holidays are processed the next business day.

3. SHIPPING COVERAGE
BrightByte currently ships to all major cities and towns across Pakistan. Remote areas may experience an additional 2-3 day delay and are subject to a Rs. 200 remote area surcharge.

4. ORDER TRACKING
Once an order ships, customers receive a tracking link via SMS and email. Tracking can also be checked anytime under "My Orders" in the account dashboard.

5. DELAYED OR LOST SHIPMENTS
If a shipment has not arrived within 3 business days of the estimated delivery date, customers should contact support. BrightByte will investigate with the courier and, if the shipment is confirmed lost, issue a full refund or free replacement within 5 business days.

6. FAILED DELIVERY ATTEMPTS
Couriers attempt delivery up to 3 times. If all attempts fail, the order is returned to BrightByte's warehouse and the customer is contacted to arrange re-delivery (additional shipping fee may apply) or a refund.

7. CASH ON DELIVERY (COD)
COD is available for orders under Rs. 50,000. A verification call may be made for first-time customers or high-value COD orders before dispatch.

8. INTERNATIONAL SHIPPING
BrightByte does not currently offer international shipping outside Pakistan.
