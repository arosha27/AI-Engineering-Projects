BRIGHTBYTE ELECTRONICS — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: How do I track my order?
A: Go to "My Orders" in your account dashboard, or use the tracking link sent to you via SMS and email once your order ships.

Q: Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?
A: Address changes are possible only before the order is processed (within the first 24 hours on business days). Contact support immediately with your order number if you need to change an address.

Q: Do you offer installment payment plans?
A: Yes, BrightByte partners with select banks to offer 0% markup installment plans on credit card purchases over Rs. 20,000, typically for 3, 6, or 12 months.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept credit/debit cards, bank transfers, JazzCash, EasyPaisa, and Cash on Delivery (COD) for eligible orders.

Q: How do I cancel an order?
A: Orders can be cancelled free of charge before they are shipped. Once shipped, the order must be refused at delivery or returned per the standard Return Policy.

Q: Is my personal information secure?
A: Yes, BrightByte uses encrypted payment processing and does not store full card details on our servers. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Q: Do you price match with other retailers?
A: BrightByte offers a price match guarantee within 7 days of purchase if the same product is found cheaper at another authorized retailer in Pakistan, subject to verification.

Q: How can I contact customer support?
A: Support is available via live chat (9 AM - 9 PM daily), email at support@brightbyte.pk, or phone at 0800-BRIGHT (0800-274448).

Q: Do you have physical stores?
A: BrightByte operates showrooms in Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad. Store locations and hours are listed on the "Store Locator" page.

Q: What should I do if I receive the wrong item?
A: Contact support within 48 hours with your order number and a photo of the item received. BrightByte will arrange a free pickup and send the correct item at no extra cost.

Q: Can I get a VAT/tax invoice for my purchase?
A: Yes, a tax invoice is automatically generated and available for download under "My Orders" for every completed purchase.
