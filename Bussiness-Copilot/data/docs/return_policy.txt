BRIGHTBYTE ELECTRONICS — RETURN & REFUND POLICY

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. RETURN WINDOW
Customers may return most products within 14 days of the delivery date for a full refund. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and include all accessories, manuals, and warranty cards.

2. NON-RETURNABLE ITEMS
The following items cannot be returned once opened:
- Earphones and headphones (for hygiene reasons)
- Software licenses and digital downloads
- Gift cards
- Items marked "Final Sale" at checkout

3. DAMAGED OR DEFECTIVE ITEMS
If a product arrives damaged or defective, customers must report it within 48 hours of delivery by contacting support with photos of the damage. Damaged items are eligible for a full refund or free replacement, and BrightByte covers return shipping costs in these cases.

4. RETURN PROCESS
Step 1: Log in to your BrightByte account and go to "My Orders."
Step 2: Select the item you wish to return and choose a reason.
Step 3: Print the prepaid return label (for defective items only; for change-of-mind returns, customers cover return shipping).
Step 4: Drop off the package at any authorized courier location within 5 business days.

5. REFUND TIMELINE
Refunds are processed within 3-5 business days after the returned item is received and inspected at our warehouse. Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Bank processing times may add an additional 3-7 business days.

6. EXCHANGES
BrightByte does not offer direct exchanges. Customers must return the original item for a refund and place a new order for the desired item.

7. RESTOCKING FEE
A 10% restocking fee applies to returned large appliances (refrigerators, washing machines, air conditioners) due to change of mind. No restocking fee applies to defective items.

8. WARRANTY CLAIMS AFTER RETURN WINDOW
For issues discovered after the 14-day return window, customers should refer to the Warranty Policy document, as manufacturer warranty terms apply instead of the standard return policy.
